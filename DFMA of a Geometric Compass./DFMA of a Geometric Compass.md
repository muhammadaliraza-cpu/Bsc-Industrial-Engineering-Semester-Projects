# DFMA of a Geometric Compass

Product redesign for simpler manufacturing, assembly, and improved usability.

## Project Overview

This project applied **Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFMA)** principles to redesign a geometric compass.

The work focused on eliminating redundant parts, optimizing key components, and standardizing critical elements to simplify production and assembly while considering usability.

## Project Objectives

- Reduce unnecessary components and manufacturing complexity.
- Simplify product assembly.
- Lower manufacturing costs and assembly time.
- Standardize critical components for consistent production.
- Improve usability through design simplification.

## Design Approach

| Design Action | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Identify and remove redundant parts | Reduce part count and simplify the product |
| Optimize key components | Make the design easier to manufacture |
| Standardize critical components | Support production consistency |
| Simplify assembly | Reduce assembly effort and cycle time |
| Consider usability | Make the compass more convenient to use |

## Reported Outcomes

- Reduced part count.
- Lower manufacturing complexity and costs.
- Improved assembly cycle time.
- Greater component standardization.
- Improved product consistency.
