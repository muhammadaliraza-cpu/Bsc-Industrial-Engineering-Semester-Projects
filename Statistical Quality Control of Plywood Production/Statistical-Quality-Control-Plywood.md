# Statistical Quality Control of Plywood Production

**An industrial case study using Microsoft Excel, control charts, and sustainable manufacturing principles to investigate production variation.**

## Project Overview

This semester project investigated quality concerns at **Haidery Plywood**, located on Haripur Road near Hattar Industrial Estate, Pakistan.

The study focused on the peeling department following reports of inconsistent plywood thickness. Production measurements from three peelers were analyzed using **Microsoft Excel** and **X̄ and R control charts** to examine process variation and develop recommendations for quality improvement.

## Problem Statement

Variability in peeling operations can affect material consistency and contribute to rejected products, rework, and unnecessary timber consumption.

The project aimed to:

- Examine measurement patterns across three peelers.
- Apply statistical techniques to assess process behavior.
- Identify areas requiring further investigation.
- Recommend maintenance and monitoring practices to support consistent production.

## Dataset

The dataset contained **120 girth measurements of poplar logs**, recorded across three peelers.

## Work Completed

### Data Organization and Analysis

Organized the measurements in Excel and calculated descriptive statistics, including averages, minimum and maximum values, and ranges.

### Control Chart Construction

Constructed **X̄ charts** to examine average measurements and **R charts** to examine variability. Plotted the observations alongside calculated control limits to support visual assessment of process behavior.

### Process Investigation

Examined measurement patterns and potential sources of variation in the peeling department. The report identified **peeler-tool wear as a suspected contributor** requiring maintenance attention.

### Improvement Recommendations

Recommended replacing worn peeler tools and introducing routine measurement and chart review to support earlier identification of process changes.

## Sustainability Perspective

The recommendations connected quality improvement with more efficient use of manufacturing resources. Their intended benefits included:

- Reducing avoidable timber waste.
- Limiting rework and repeated processing.
- Improving tool maintenance practices.
- Supporting consistent production through ongoing monitoring.

## Tools and Methods

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data organization, calculations, and charting.
- **Statistical Process Control:** X̄ and R control charts.
- **Descriptive Statistics:** Mean, range, and measurement comparisons.
- **Sustainable Manufacturing:** Waste prevention and resource efficiency.
